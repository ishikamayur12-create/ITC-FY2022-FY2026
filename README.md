 ITC Financial Analysis (FY22-FY26)

Power BI dashboard on five years of ITC Ltd sales, profit, margins and borrowings.

**Tools:** Power BI

## Key findings
- **Sales:** record ₹78,868 Cr in FY26, but growth has slowed from 23.12% in FY22 to 4.71% in FY26
- **Margin:** operating margin peaked at 37.08% in FY24 and has stayed in a stable 34% to 37% band
- **FY25 profit:** net profit of ₹35,052 Cr includes ₹15,155 Cr of one-time exceptional income, so underlying profit was about ₹19,900 Cr (approximate)
- **FY26 profit fall (-40.04%):** caused by the high FY25 base, not weaker operations, as operating profit rose from ₹25,832 Cr to ₹27,306 Cr
- **Debt:** borrowings rose to ₹2,399 Cr in FY26, mainly short-term, but are only about 3% of sales
